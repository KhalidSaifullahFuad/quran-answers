# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented here.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-01

### New
- 200 questions about Islam, each answered with the Quran ayahs that prove it, in Bengali
- Full Arabic mushaf with Bengali translation; open any cited ayah in its surrounding context
- Browse by 14 topics, by surah, or the full question list
- Search in Bengali or Arabic, by words or by reference (e.g. 2:255)
- Bookmark ayahs into folders; reading history you can clear
- Share a question and its answer
- Light and dark themes, bundled Arabic and Bengali fonts
- Works fully offline: no internet permission, no ads, no data collected
