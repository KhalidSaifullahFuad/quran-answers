# Hedging words in ayah translations — review (2026-11-02)

Source: `content/quran.json` (`translationBn`). Search: `সম্ভবত | আশা কর | হয়ত` → 34 ayat.
"যদি থাকে" excluded per author.

Rule used for proposals:
- **عسى from Allah** = certain (ابن عباس: «عسى من الله واجبة») → "অবশ্যই".
- **لعلّ after a command/sign from Allah** = purpose (তা'লীল) → "যাতে …".
- **Human speaker** (Musa, Ibrahim, a believer, etc.) → keep hope wording; he did not know.
- **"আশা" as real verb** (طمع / رجا / ودّ) → keep, not a hedge.

Resolved column: author fills (✅ apply / ✏️ edit / ❌ keep).
