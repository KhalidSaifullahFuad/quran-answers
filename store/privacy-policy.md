# Privacy Policy — Quran Answers

*Last updated: 19 October 2026*

Quran Answers (`com.khalidfuad.quran_answers`) does not collect, store on any
server, share or sell any personal or usage data.

- **No network access.** The app does not request the Android internet
  permission and makes no network connections. All content — the Qur'an text,
  the translation and the questions — ships inside the app.
- **No accounts, ads, analytics or crash reporting.** No third-party SDKs that
  collect data are included.
- **Data kept on your device only.** Your bookmarks, folders, reading history
  and display settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  They never leave the device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data in
  Android settings, deletes them. Reading history can also be cleared from the
  app's Saved tab.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address
before the change takes effect.
